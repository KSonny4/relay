Relay

Developers ship the product, then lose the room.

They find out the pitch failed after they sit down.

Relay is the rehearsal.

You talk for as long as you need.

The transcript runs in front of you.

A score moves while you are still speaking: how well this pitch explains an idea that helps developers.

You press stop when you are done.

A long pause with no words locks it too.

Then one sentence: the next line to fix.

The recording and the session stay, so the next take can be better.

I am pitching Relay on Relay.

A developer should leave knowing what it is, why it helps, and what to do next.

Talk.

Watch the score move.

Stop.
